Your conversation, step by step

  1. Choose Hindi or Telugu. The doctor speaks English. Live microphone uses your voice; rehearsal plays example text.
  2. Start session and allow the microphone. Patient listening starts automatically. With one microphone, tap the other speaker’s microphone button to switch.
  3. Read Live conversation below. “Heard” shows speech as it is recognized; “Translation” shows the other language. Completed turns stay in the conversation history.
  4. Watch Automatic attention. Sustained loud speech raises an intensity flag; explicit breathing-distress phrases raise an attention flag. These signals require clinician confirmation.
  5. End session at the top. This stops both microphones. Export saves the completed bilingual turns.
